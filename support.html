A little help.

How does cleanup work?

Swipe right to keep a photo or video. Swipe left to set it aside. Everything you set aside waits in Review, where you can look again and change your mind.

To delete the items in Review, tap “Move to Recently Deleted” and confirm in Picko and in the iOS prompt. You can recover deleted items from Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions sync to your other devices.

How does the daily goal work?

Picko is free. Your goal is 50 swipes on your first day and 30 a day after that. When you reach it, tap “Keep going” to keep sorting for as long as you like.

Keeping or setting aside a photo counts toward your goal. Undo reverses that decision and its count.

What is Largest First?

After your first 20 decisions in Random and once Picko has finished measuring your library, Largest First opens. It shows your biggest measured photos and videos first, so you can decide which large files to keep, compress or send to Review.

Why is a size missing?

Some originals live only in iCloud. Picko does not download them in the background just to measure them, so their size can show as unavailable. That does not mean the file is empty. The total on screen is the size of the media, not a promise of how much free space your iPhone will report.

Photo access

You can give Picko all your photos or only the ones you pick. With selected photos, Picko works on those, and “Add more” on the home screen lets you share more. Change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Picko.
