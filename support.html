How does cleanup work?

Swipe right to keep a photo or video. Swipe left to set it aside. Everything you set aside waits in Review, where you can look again and change your mind.

Nothing is deleted until you tap Delete in Review and then confirm in the iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions sync to your other devices.

How many swipes do I get?

Picko is free. You get 50 swipes on your first day and 30 each day after that. Keeping or setting aside one photo counts as one swipe. Undo gives the swipe back.

Viewing photos, undo, Compress and finishing Review never use swipes.

What is Largest First?

After your first 20 swipes and once Picko has finished measuring your library, Largest First opens. It shows your biggest photos and videos first, so each swipe frees more space.

Why is a size missing?

Some originals live only in iCloud. Picko does not download them in the background just to measure them, so their size can show as unavailable. That does not mean the file is empty. The total on screen is the size of the media, not a promise of how much free space your iPhone will report.

Photo access

You can give Picko all your photos or only the ones you pick. With selected photos, Picko works on those, and “Add more” on the home screen lets you share more. Change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Picko.
