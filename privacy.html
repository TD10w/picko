Privacy

Your photos. Your business.

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Your photo library

Picko analyzes photos and videos on your iPhone. It does not upload your library to the developer. You choose what Picko can see: all photos, or only the ones you pick. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings.

Saved on your device

Picko saves your browsing progress, Keep and Review lists, cleanup history, analysis results and preferences in its own app storage. These records can include photo identifiers and metadata. They let you continue where you left off and are not sent to the developer.

Apple services

When a photo with a location is on screen or you open its details, its coordinates may be sent to Apple’s location service to look up a place name. If Apple has no answer, Picko picks the nearest city from a list built into the app, without sending anything. Apple Photos may retrieve media from iCloud when it is needed for viewing or for an action you choose.

Purchases

This version of Picko is free and offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Feedback

In-app feedback is turned off in this version. Picko does not send feedback reports. If you email support, we receive only what you choose to send.

No account, no ads, no tracking

Picko has no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking.

Place names

The built-in city list comes from GeoNames (geonames.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Your choices

You can change photo access in iOS Settings. Removing Picko removes its app storage. Review your selections before confirming any deletion; Apple Photos manages Recently Deleted and iCloud syncing.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email Xin Du at pick.pony@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
